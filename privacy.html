1. Voditelj obrade

Za sva pitanja o zaštiti podataka pišite nam na info@rdd.software.

2. Koje podatke obrađujemo i zašto

2.1. Posjet stranici (hosting)

Stranica se poslužuje putem usluge GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., SAD), a promet prolazi kroz Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., SAD) radi brzine i zaštite od napada. Pri svakom posjetu ti pružatelji usluga automatski obrađuju tehničke podatke: IP adresu, datum i vrijeme pristupa, zatraženu stranicu, vrstu preglednika i operativnog sustava. Ti su podaci nužni da bi se stranica uopće prikazala i zaštitila. Pravna osnova: legitimni interes (čl. 6. st. 1. t. (f) GDPR-a).

2.2. Automatski odabir jezika

Kod prvog posjeta, ako jezik niste sami odabrali, vaš preglednik šalje upit servisu GeoJS (get.geojs.io), koji iz vaše IP adrese određuje samo državu. Na temelju države stranicu prikazujemo na hrvatskom, engleskom, talijanskom ili njemačkom jeziku. Mi ne primamo niti pohranjujemo vašu IP adresu ni državu — u vašem pregledniku spremamo samo odabrani jezik. Ako jezik odaberete ručno, upit se više ne šalje. Pravna osnova: legitimni interes da vam stranicu prikažemo na razumljivom jeziku (čl. 6. st. 1. t. (f) GDPR-a).

2.3. Analitika (Google Analytics)

Koristimo Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited, Irska) kako bismo razumjeli koliko posjetitelja imamo i koje ih stranice zanimaju. Google Analytics postavlja kolačiće (_ga, _ga_*) i prikuplja podatke o korištenju stranice, uređaju i približnoj lokaciji. Google Analytics 4 ne pohranjuje IP adrese. Podaci se mogu prenositi Googleu u SAD. Pravna osnova: vaša privola (čl. 6. st. 1. t. (a) GDPR-a), koju možete povući u bilo kojem trenutku. Podaci se u Google Analyticsu čuvaju najviše 14 mjeseci.

2.4. Fontovi

Fontovi stranice učitavaju se s poslužitelja Google Fonts (Google Ireland Limited), pri čemu Google vidi vašu IP adresu. Pravna osnova: legitimni interes za ujednačen prikaz stranice (čl. 6. st. 1. t. (f) GDPR-a).

2.5. Kontakt e-poštom

Kada nam pišete e-poštom, obrađujemo vašu adresu e-pošte, ime i sadržaj poruke kako bismo vam odgovorili i, ako to želite, pripremili ponudu. Pravna osnova: poduzimanje radnji na vaš zahtjev prije sklapanja ugovora (čl. 6. st. 1. t. (b) GDPR-a) i legitimni interes za odgovaranje na upite (čl. 6. st. 1. t. (f) GDPR-a). Poruke čuvamo dok je to potrebno za komunikaciju, a ako iz upita nastane poslovni odnos, onoliko koliko to propisuju računovodstveni i porezni propisi.

2.6. Postavke u vašem pregledniku

U lokalnoj pohrani vašeg preglednika (localStorage) spremamo samo vaše postavke: odabrani jezik (rdd-lang), automatski određeni jezik (rdd-lang-geo) i tamni ili svijetli način (rdd-theme). Ti podaci ostaju na vašem uređaju, ne šalju se nama ni trećim stranama i možete ih obrisati u postavkama preglednika.

3. Primatelji i prijenos izvan EU

Podatke obrađuju pružatelji usluga navedeni gore (GitHub, Cloudflare, GeoJS, Google), isključivo za opisane svrhe. Neki od njih nalaze se u SAD-u ili podatke obrađuju u SAD-u; prijenos se temelji na Okviru za privatnost podataka EU-a i SAD-a (EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework) ili na standardnim ugovornim klauzulama Europske komisije. Vaše podatke ne prodajemo i ne koristimo ih za automatizirano donošenje odluka ni izradu profila.

4. Vaša prava

U skladu s GDPR-om imate pravo:

  • na pristup svojim podacima i na njihov ispravak,
  • na brisanje podataka i ograničenje obrade,
  • na prigovor obradi koja se temelji na legitimnom interesu,
  • na prenosivost podataka,
  • povući privolu u bilo kojem trenutku, bez utjecaja na zakonitost obrade prije povlačenja.

Zahtjev nam pošaljite na info@rdd.software; odgovorit ćemo najkasnije u roku od mjesec dana. Imate i pravo podnijeti pritužbu Agenciji za zaštitu osobnih podataka (AZOP), Selska cesta 136, 10000 Zagreb, azop.hr.

5. Izmjene izjave

Ovu izjavu možemo povremeno izmijeniti, primjerice kad uvedemo novu uslugu na stranici. Važeća verzija uvijek je objavljena na ovoj stranici, s datumom posljednjeg ažuriranja.

This policy explains which personal data we process when you visit rdd.software or contact us, for what purposes, on what legal basis, and what your rights are. Processing is carried out in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and the Croatian Act on the Implementation of the GDPR.

1. Data controller

R.D.D., obrt za računalno programiranje, vl. Milan Trbojević, Mihovilići 2b, 51000 Rijeka, Croatia, OIB (Croatian tax ID): 09887105714 (“RDD”, “we”).
For any data protection questions, write to info@rdd.software.

2. What data we process and why

2.1. Visiting the website (hosting)

The website is served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA), and traffic passes through Cloudflare (Cloudflare, Inc., USA) for speed and protection against attacks. On every visit these providers automatically process technical data: IP address, date and time of access, the requested page, browser and operating system type. This data is necessary to display and protect the website. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

2.2. Automatic language selection

On your first visit, if you have not chosen a language yourself, your browser sends a request to the GeoJS service (get.geojs.io), which determines only your country from your IP address. Based on the country, we show the site in Croatian, English, Italian or German. We do not receive or store your IP address or country — only the chosen language is saved in your browser. If you choose a language manually, the request is no longer sent. Legal basis: legitimate interest in showing you the site in a language you understand (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

2.3. Analytics (Google Analytics)

We use Google Analytics 4 (Google Ireland Limited, Ireland) to understand how many visitors we have and which pages interest them. Google Analytics sets cookies (_ga, _ga_*) and collects data about site usage, your device and approximate location. Google Analytics 4 does not store IP addresses. Data may be transferred to Google in the USA. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time. Data is kept in Google Analytics for at most 14 months.

2.4. Fonts

The website fonts are loaded from Google Fonts servers (Google Ireland Limited), which means Google sees your IP address. Legal basis: legitimate interest in a consistent presentation of the website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

2.5. Contact by email

When you email us, we process your email address, name and the content of your message to reply and, if you wish, to prepare an offer. Legal basis: steps taken at your request prior to entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and legitimate interest in answering enquiries (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We keep messages as long as needed for the conversation and, if a business relationship results, for as long as accounting and tax regulations require.

2.6. Settings in your browser

In your browser's local storage (localStorage) we save only your settings: the chosen language (rdd-lang), the automatically detected language (rdd-lang-geo) and dark or light mode (rdd-theme). This data stays on your device, is not sent to us or to third parties, and you can delete it in your browser settings.

3. Recipients and transfers outside the EU

Data is processed by the service providers listed above (GitHub, Cloudflare, GeoJS, Google) solely for the purposes described. Some of them are located in or process data in the USA; such transfers are based on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework or on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. We do not sell your data or use it for automated decision-making or profiling.

4. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • access your data and have it rectified,
  • have your data erased and its processing restricted,
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest,
  • data portability,
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal.

Send your request to info@rdd.software; we will reply within one month at the latest. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP), Selska cesta 136, 10000 Zagreb, azop.hr, or with the supervisory authority in your country.

5. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when we add a new service to the website. The current version is always published on this page with the date of the last update.
